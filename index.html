About Aicha

Computer Science Student @ 42 Network • Machine Learning & AI Engineer

I’m a Computer Science student at 42 Network with a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. My journey started with mathematics, algorithms and software engineering, and gradually evolved toward building intelligent systems—from AI algorithms and reinforcement learning agents to neural networks and data-driven models.

What motivates me most is understanding how intelligent systems work and turning that understanding into something I can actually build. I enjoy going beyond using existing tools and digging into the algorithms, mathematics and reasoning behind them.

I’m currently developing my skills across Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP and data-driven AI while exploring the intersection between artificial intelligence and neuroscience. My long-term ambition is to specialize in Brain-Computer Interfaces and neurotechnology, using AI to create systems that can interact more naturally with the human brain.

This portfolio is a collection of the projects, experiments and ideas that represent that journey—from the algorithms I built from scratch to the AI systems I continue to explore.

Machine Learning & AI

From custom Multilayer Perceptron neural networks and Q-Learning reinforcement agents (Learn2Slither) to gradient descent optimization (ft_linear_regression) and statistical data modeling (DSLR).

Software & Startups
